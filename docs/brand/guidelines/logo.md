# Logo

Files in `docs/brand/logo/` — all SVGs are outlined paths (no font dependency).

- `wordmark.svg` — primary (W1): shikkoku letters + moegi on-air dot. Use on
  byakuroku/gofun grounds. On voice-tinted grounds the dot goes paper (see
  wordmark-mono recolored, or the cover system).
- `wordmark-mono.svg` — single-color (`currentColor`): recolor freely; ink on
  light, paper on dark/tinted.
- `wordmark-watermark.svg` — quiet W3 (no dot, 40% opacity) for covers, share
  footers, legal.
- `mark.svg` — M1 monogram (o + dot) where the wordmark won't fit.
- `seal.svg` — M3 secondary 音 seal. Only: about screen, share watermark,
  loading. Never below 16px, never red, never every screen.

Clear space: the dot's diameter on all sides. Don't: stretch, outline, shadow,
gradient-fill, set in any other typeface, or add "on-air" copy next to the dot.
