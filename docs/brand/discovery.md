# oto — brand discovery (Phase 1, approved answers)

Interview date: 2026-08-21. Status: **approved 2026-08-21**.

## Taste profile

1. **Emotional register — Morning radio.** Bright, energetic, alive. Your daily
   brief read to you while you make coffee. Upbeat and utilitarian — not
   late-night intimacy, not studio-tool seriousness.
2. **Era — Futuristic-editorial.** Editorial structure (strong type, whitespace,
   clear hierarchy) set in modern/geometric letterforms with techy details.
   "A magazine from 2035, not 1965." Clean and futuristic overall.
3. **Color philosophy — Voice-mapped family.** The per-voice orb hues ARE the
   brand palette. A small family of vivid colors, each meaning a voice,
   surfacing in the player, artwork, and sharing surfaces. Semantic mapping:
   color = voice (flat set, not an ordered ladder).
4. **Base scheme — True light + true dark.** Both modes equally canonical,
   designed as a pair. No primary mode. (Current app: warm cream light /
   warm charcoal dark — a starting point, not a constraint.)
5. **Heritage — Japanese, present but subtle.** oto = 音 (sound). Findable,
   never a theme: e.g. 音 as a hidden mark, Japanese-named colors, ma
   (negative space) as a layout principle.
6. **Wordmark routes to explore (all three):**
   - Palindrome symmetry — o-t-o mirror lettering, the t as center axis.
   - Editorial type + twist — strong typeface, lowercase, one custom detail.
   - The o's as sound — the two o's become speaker/orb/wave shapes.
7. **Numerals — Quiet UI digits.** Durations and counts are metadata, not
   heroes. Same type as body, small, unobtrusive.
8. **References:** clean + futuristic in general; Apple Podcasts / Overcast
   for native iOS polish. (No retro, no hardware nostalgia.)

## Banned list (never present anything in this territory)

- Purple-blue AI gradients / "AI product" sparkle look.
- Skeuomorphic audio: waveform clip-art, mic icons, cassettes, vinyl, EQ bars
  as decoration.
- Corporate SaaS blue / Inter-on-white sameness.
- Cute mascots / character illustrations.

## Existing de-facto identity (context)

- iOS `Theme.swift`: warm paper/ink neutrals (light 0xf6f4ee bg / dark 0x23211e),
  amber accent (0xe08600 / 0xffb02e).
- `OrbPalettes.swift`: per-voice 3-color liquid orb palettes (alloy silver-blue,
  ash ember, ballad rose, coral sun, echo teal, fable violet, nova ice-blue,
  onyx graphite…). These orbs are the strongest existing brand asset.
