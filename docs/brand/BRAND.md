# oto — brand

**Tagline:** Anything, read aloud.
**Identity:** Chōkan 朝刊 (futuristic-editorial morning edition) on byakuroku 白緑
sage-cast paper, moegi 萌黄 accent. Approved 2026-08-21 — full trail in
[decisions.md](decisions.md), tokens in [tokens.json](tokens.json).

## Thesis

oto is morning radio for anything you can paste: bright, energetic, utilitarian —
the daily brief read to you while you make coffee. The look is a magazine from
2035: editorial structure (mastheads, eyebrows, display/dek stacks, generous
deliberate space) set in modern geometric type. The color system is the product's
own: **voices own the color** — every voice has one canonical hue named for a
traditional Japanese color, doing for oto what album art does for music apps,
with colors we author instead of extract.

## The three rules that settle arguments

1. **Voice colors never travel alone.** An initial glyph or name always rides
   along (the Tokyo Metro rule). Chrome stays byakuroku/ink; voice color appears
   where a voice is present — tiles, orbs, covers, progress.
2. **Gradients are tonal only.** A hue may ramp its own lightness. Cross-hue
   lives only in the liquid orbs and shimmer (tamamushi, iridescent by
   definition). A purple-to-blue wash is the one thing oto will never ship.
3. **Real words doing a job.** Japanese appears only where it is true and
   functional: color names (朱 shu), the 音 seal (it *is* the name), ma as
   spacing and timing. The moment it becomes ornament, cut it.

## Naming

- Voices keep their vendor ids (coral, echo…). The Japanese name belongs to the
  **color**, not the voice: "coral · 朱 shu". Kanji as micro-label, romaji
  everywhere else.
- New voices take the nearest free dentōshoku anchor; yamabuki 山吹 stays
  reserved (the retired gold — no voice takes the old accent).
- Feature names may draw on giongo (real sound-mimetic words, romaji, meaning
  must fit) — never katakana in UI, never a word a native speaker would find odd.

## Copy voice

Morning-radio plain: short declaratives, product truth, zero AI-hype adjectives.
"Paste a text. Pick a voice. It's yours forever." Numbers are quiet metadata
(tabular, muted), never heroes. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

## The quiet layer

- **Ma 間** — deliberate intervals: editorial margins, a beat of silence before
  playback, a soft settle after. Never serene-but-slow.
- **A440 → A880** — all chimes build on this octave (the NHK jihou homage).
- **音** appears in exactly three places: about screen, share-page watermark,
  loading state. Never below 16px.
