oto
No dot — for watermarks, legal, co-branding where the accent would fight.

oto
Dot inside the final o's counter. Clever once, cluttered always — the counter is the sound aperture; filling it mutes it.

09The quiet layer

  • Ma 間 — the interval. Editorial margins are deliberate; playback begins after a beat of silence and settles softly at the end. Space and pause mean something; never serene-but-slow.
  • A440 → A880. All app chimes (generation complete, player ready) build on the A4→A5 octave — the NHK jihou homage. Findable by ear, invisible to the eye.
  • 音 appears on the about screen, share-page watermark, and loading state only. Kanji color-names ride as micro-labels (朱, 浅葱…). Romaji everywhere else. Real words, real jobs — the moment any of it becomes ornament, cut it.
