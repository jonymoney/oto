# oto brand — phase decisions log

## Phase 2 — candidate directions (2026-08-21)

Presented: **Chōkan** 朝刊 (futuristic-editorial morning edition), **Rosen** 路線
(wayfinding/roundel system), **Kagami** 鏡 (palindrome + ma). Archived page:
`review-phase2.html`.

**Winner — merge:**
- Base direction: **Chōkan** (editorial structure, Clash Display + SF Pro/Instrument
  Sans, voice colors as chromatic tiles, morning-edition energy).
- Wordmark: **Chōkan's own "oto + on-air dot"** (lowercase Clash Display).
  The user first named "the logo of Rosen" but clarified in Phase 2.5 that they
  meant the Chōkan mark — the roundel wordmark is rejected.
- **sage / 萌黄 moegi** is a loved color — feature it prominently.

**Rejections:**
- Kagami 鏡 — not selected (no stated objection; too quiet relative to the
  morning-radio register).
- Rosen's roundel wordmark (ring-t-ring) — picked by name at the Phase 2 gate,
  then rejected on sight in Phase 2.5 ("fix the logo — I want the Chōkan one").

## Phase 2.5 — ground tests (2026-08-21)

The current light ground tends brown/yellow; five grounds tested (current paper,
gofun, geppaku, byakuroku, aijiro) with matched dark twins. Archived page:
`review-phase25.html`.

**Winner: Byakuroku 白緑** — sage-cast paper `#f4f7ee`, dark twin `#181a13`,
**with moegi 萌黄 as the brand accent** (the byakuroku card demoed moegi accents
and was picked as shown; yamabuki gold retires from the accent role). sage the
voice deliberately shares the house hue — sage is the house voice.

**Rejections:** current warm paper (tends brown/yellow — the reason for the round),
gofun (fine but anonymous next to byakuroku), geppaku and aijiro (cool casts — not
chosen, no stated objection).
