Choosing notes

Ground + accent are separable. Any ground can pair with either accent (yamabuki gold or moegi sage) — byakuroku just demos the sage-accent world. Name a combination and it becomes the Phase 3 spec, e.g. "geppaku ground, moegi accent". Dark twins are graded to each cast, so the pair stays true in both modes.
