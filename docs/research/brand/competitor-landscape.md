# oto — competitor visual-identity landscape

Researched 2026-08-21. What each brand actually looks like today (palette, type, motion, dark handling), then implications for oto. Hexes are approximate unless from official/brand-asset sources.

---

## 1. Named references (native iOS polish)

### Apple Podcasts
- **Palette**: brand purple/violet gradient icon (~#8940FB → #C566F0 range); in-app accent is system purple. Since iOS 26, the app is Liquid Glass: translucent panels that *sample color from show artwork* on the Now Playing screen — content supplies the color, chrome stays glass.
- **Type**: SF Pro throughout. Large-title navigation, standard iOS hierarchy.
- **Motion**: system-native — blurred glass panels over scrolling art, spring transitions, artwork scale on play.
- **Dark**: true system light/dark; glass adapts, artwork color persists across both.
- Sources: https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-26-podcasts/ · https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/apple-introduces-a-delightful-and-elegant-new-software-design/

### Overcast
- **Palette**: signature orange (~#FC7E0F) as the single brand accent. 2022 redesign: **orange light mode / blue dark mode as a designed pair**, plus a picker of alternate tints. Playlists get user-chosen colors + SF Symbols icons.
- **Type**: San Francisco **Rounded** — the friendliness lever. No custom typeface.
- **Motion**: restrained, native; utility over spectacle.
- **Dark**: the light/dark *pair with different hues* is the notable move — dark is not "light dimmed," it's a sibling identity.
- Sources: https://marco.org/2022/03/25/overcast-redesign-2022 · https://www.macrumors.com/2022/03/25/overcast-ios-podcast-player-major-update/

## 2. TTS / AI-voice products

### ElevenLabs
- **Palette**: warm-white minimalism — eggshell canvas ~#FDFCFC, warm taupe surfaces ~#F5F3F1, black type. Logo strictly black/white. Two accents — vivid blue **#0447FF** and vivid orange **#FF4704** — appear *only inside product visuals* (audio spheres), never as UI chrome.
- **Type**: Waldenburg at whisper-weight 300 for display (tight −0.02em), Inter 400/500 for everything else. Research-lab austerity.
- **Details**: hairline 1px borders, 20px card radii, fully-pilled buttons, sub-0.1-opacity shadows. The "audio object" is an abstract orb/sphere — notably **no waveforms as decoration**.
- **Dark**: marketing/product lean light-first; the identity is the off-white.
- Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/brand · https://styles.refero.design/style/031056ff-7af1-46db-8daa-115f731c5d26 · https://www.shadcn.io/design/elevenlabs

### Speechify
- **Palette**: cobalt/royal blue as brand color; official guidance is monochrome black/white logo for most uses, blue only on white for emphasis. Reads as productivity-SaaS blue.
- **Type**: ABC Diatype (workhorse sans) + Recoleta (boutique soft serif) — a warm-serif/neutral-sans pairing from their design-language refresh; they deliberately dropped earlier wave/waveform elements.
- **Motion/dark**: app is utilitarian light-first with standard dark mode; no strong motion identity.
- Sources: https://speechify.com/brand-kit/ · https://maaahe.com/New-design-language

### Play.ht
- **Status**: effectively gone — after Meta's 2025 PlayAI acquisition, PlayHT products/APIs were discontinued. Historically a dark, gradient-accented AI-SaaS look. Not a live reference.
- Source: https://10web.io/ai-tools/play-ht/

### OpenAI voice (ChatGPT)
- **Visual**: the voice identity is the **fluid blue-white orb** — a breathing sky-like blob (evolved from black dots). 2025 update moved voice inline into the chat stream, shrinking the orb from full-screen ritual to ambient element. Brand around it: black/white, OpenAI Sans, near-zero color.
- **Read**: this orb IS the current AI-voice cliché — soft blue/purple luminous blob on dark.
- Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/24/openai-rolls-out-advanced-voice-mode-with-more-voices-and-a-new-look · https://www.webpronews.com/the-death-of-the-overlay-how-openais-integrated-voice-mode-signals-the-end-of-static-computing/

## 3. Audio-consumption editorial apps

### NYT Audio
- **Look**: black-canvas editorial — white NYT Franklin/Cheltenham type on near-black, shows presented as bold color-blocked tiles; the *typography is the brand*, newspaper masthead DNA. The standalone app has been folded back into the core NYT app's "Listen" tab (five-tab nav: Home, Listen, Play, Sections, You).
- **Dark**: dark-first by design (a nighttime/commute product), not a theme toggle.
- Sources: https://www.inma.org/blogs/product-initiative/post.cfm/new-york-times-updates-its-app-design-to-be-more-personalised · https://www.fastcompany.com/91199198/the-new-york-times-app-redesign

### Pocket Casts
- **Palette**: brand red (~#F43E37) retired from chrome in v7 (red → black); in-app color is **dynamic, extracted from podcast artwork** per show. Ships a theme menu (Light, Dark, Rosé, Indigo, Electric…). Adopted Liquid Glass June 2026.
- **Type**: system SF/Roboto; no typographic identity to speak of.
- **Dark**: multiple dark variants via theme menu — user preference, not a designed pair.
- Sources: https://blog.pocketcasts.com/2026/06/11/liquid-glass/ · https://support.pocketcasts.com/knowledge-base/appearance-settings/ · https://pocketcastsv7.wordpress.com/2018/11/17/pocket-casts-v7-deep-dive-core-design-navigation/

### Spotify (podcast surfaces)
- **Palette**: green #1DB954/#1ED760 on blacks (#191414, surfaces #121212). Encore design system is **dark-first, rooted in album art** — podcast/show headers take artwork-derived gradient washes.
- **Type**: Spotify Mix (2024 custom font) — softer than the old Circular, tuned for legibility on dark screens; rolled out across all platforms.
- **Motion**: content-card carousels, artwork-derived gradients animating on Now Playing; Wrapped is the maximal-color annual exception.
- Sources: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2024-05-22/introducing-spotify-mix-our-new-and-exclusive-font/ · https://www.eggradients.com/blog/spotify-colors

### Snipd
- **Look** (observed; no published brand system found): dark-first AI-podcast player, indigo/blue-purple gradient logo and accents, glowing AI-highlight moments on transcripts, rounded cards. Sits squarely in the purple-blue-AI-gradient territory oto has banned.
- Sources: https://www.snipd.app/blog/new-snip-design-release · https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snipd-ai-podcast-player/id1557206126

## 4. Futuristic-editorial adjacent brands

### Arc / The Browser Company
- **Palette**: cream **#FFFCEC** ("Arc Offwhite" — paper, not screen) carrying hero/body/footer, with saturated #3139FB blue, #FF5060 coral, #2702C2. Sunset 2025 (→ Dia, Atlassian acquisition) but still the reference for warm-paper + vivid-accent consumer software.
- **Type**: the instructive part — a *stacked editorial system*: Marlin Soft SQ display, Inter body, ABC Oracle deks, **ABC Favorit Mono eyebrows**, Exposure VAR feature heads. Mono eyebrows + display/dek hierarchy = magazine structure in an app brand.
- Sources: https://www.shadcn.io/design/arc · https://www.loftlyy.com/en/arc-browser

### Perplexity
- **Palette**: three tones — Offblack, Paper White, **True Turquoise (~#20808D)**. Deliberately "invisible brand": quiet, bookish, anti-hype.
- **Type**: FK Grotesk, lowercase wordmark; asterisk-like geometric mark with layered meanings (cursor, citation, open book).
- **Read**: proof that an AI product can own a *non-purple*, editorial-calm identity.
- Sources: https://medium.com/smith-diction/branding-perplexity-ai-70eb2cb2ef48 · https://brandarchive.xyz/identity/perplexity

### Airbuds / Retro
- **Airbuds**: social-music widget; candy-bright reactions and sticker energy over album art, TikTok-like single-song feed; identity is playful chaos, youth-coded.
- **Retro**: warm, quiet, film-nostalgia minimalism (cream, soft type, photo-first). Both show consumer-social warmth, neither is editorial/systematic.
- Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/airbuds-widget/id1638906106 · https://refero.design/apps/150

## Implications for oto

**The banned clichés are exactly what dominates, so avoiding them is differentiation, not risk:**
1. **Purple-blue AI gradient + luminous orb on dark** = the AI-voice default (OpenAI's orb, Snipd, most TTS SaaS). It now reads "generic AI feature," not "product."
2. **Corporate/SaaS blue** = Speechify's lane (and old Play.ht). Reads utility-tool, zero warmth.
3. **Skeuomorphic audio decoration** — waveforms/mics survive only in low-end TTS tools; every premium brand here (ElevenLabs, Apple, Spotify) already dropped them. The ban aligns with the top of the market.

**Whitespace nobody occupies:**
1. **Voice-owned color.** Every player either borrows color from *content artwork* (Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Spotify) or holds one brand accent (Overcast orange, Perplexity turquoise). **No one maps a vivid color family to voices** — in oto, voices ARE the content, so oto can do artwork-derived-color's job with colors it owns. Genuinely unclaimed.
2. **Futuristic-editorial structure in an audio app.** Editorial type systems exist (NYT Audio — but B&W newspaper heritage; Arc — but a browser, and dead). Nobody in audio has magazine hierarchy: display/dek/mono-eyebrow stacks, grid discipline, techy micro-labels. Arc's stacked type system + Perplexity's restraint are the models; no audio product does it.
3. **True light + true dark as designed twins.** The space is dark-first (Spotify, NYT Audio, Snipd), light-first (ElevenLabs, Speechify), or theme-menu (Pocket Casts). Only Overcast's orange-light/blue-dark hints at a *deliberate pair* — oto can make the pair a first-class brand idea ("morning broadcast / evening broadcast").
4. **Japanese heritage layer.** Zero presence anywhere in this landscape. Even a subtle 音 layer (proportions, hairline rules, vertical micro-labels) is uncontested.
5. **Bright-energetic register.** ElevenLabs went austere lab-white; NYT went somber black; AI tools went moody dark. "Morning-radio bright" — warm paper (cf. Arc's #FFFCEC as precedent) with vivid voice hues — has no direct competitor in TTS/audio.

**Nearest-collision warnings:** ElevenLabs owns austere warm-white + light-weight display type (oto's light mode must be brighter/more chromatic, not another eggshell lab); Perplexity owns quiet-turquoise editorial calm (oto's energy level should sit well above it); artwork-tinted glass is now Apple's system default (voice-mapped color must feel authored, not extracted).
