How it works

From text to podcast in one sentence.

  1. Connect oto to Claude

    In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector and paste __MCP_URL__. Two minutes, free.

  2. Say "read this aloud"

    Paste an article, drop in your notes, or just ask a question and have the answer narrated. Pick one of 19 voices, or let it pick.

  3. Press play — and keep it

    The player appears right in the conversation, and the audio lands in your library forever. Nothing expires, nothing regenerates.

Questions

Before you ask.

Can Claude read its answers out loud?

Yes. With oto connected, ask Claude to read any answer aloud and you get a saved, replayable audio in your library — not a one-off voice that disappears when the chat ends.

How do I turn an article into a podcast?

Paste the article (or its link) into Claude and ask for it read aloud. oto turns it into an episode in your library that you can play like a podcast — while you cook, commute, or train.

Is there a free plan?

Yes — 10 minutes of generated audio every month, no card required. Unlimited generation is $9.99/month, cancel anytime.

Do I need to install an app?

No. oto works inside Claude on the web and desktop. An iOS app is on the way. Just confirmed your email? Head back to your chat and reconnect — sign-in picks up where you left off.

Who can hear my audio?

Your library is private by default. Every audio has a share page that only people with the link can play — no account needed to listen.
